## Overview

Cato is a local-first daily self-tracking app. It helps you log habits, mood, routines, media attachments, and reminders on your Android device.

Cato is not a medical device, healthcare provider, diagnosis tool, or treatment service. The app is for personal self-tracking only.

## Data Stored on Your Device

Cato stores the following data locally on your device:

- Tracker entries, including dates, times, scores, counts, notes, selections, and routine completion.
- Review history, heatmap data, streaks, and daily score calculations derived from your entries.
- Media attachments you choose to add through the camera or gallery.
- App settings, including theme, typography, sound, haptic, notification, and reminder preferences.
- Export files you choose to create.

This data is stored in app-private storage on your device. For this release, Cato does not require an account and does not sync your tracker data to a cloud account.

## Data Sent Off Your Device

For this release, Cato does not send your tracker entries, notes, scores, media attachments, reminders, or exported files to a Cato backend.

Cato may use Sentry for crash and error diagnostics if enabled in the release build. Sentry may receive crash reports, error details, device and app diagnostics, operating system information, app version, and technical breadcrumbs needed to understand and fix app failures. Cato configures Sentry not to send default personally identifiable information and not to attach screenshots or view hierarchy data.

Cato includes Firebase Core initialization for future platform services. For this release, Cato does not use Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Cloud Messaging, or Cloud Functions for app behavior.

## Camera, Gallery, and Media

Cato asks for camera access only when you choose to capture a media attachment. You may also choose an existing item from your gallery. Media attachments are stored locally in app-private storage unless you intentionally export or share them using Android system features.

## Notifications

Cato uses local notifications for reminders. Reminder times and notification preferences are stored on your device. Cato does not use remote push notifications in this release.

## Data Export and Sharing

Cato lets you export your data as JSON. Exporting is initiated by you. If you choose to share an export file with another app or service, that sharing is handled by Android and the selected destination's privacy practices.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your local Cato data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the app, remove exported files, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling Cato deletes app-private local data according to Android system behavior.

Cato does not offer account creation in this release, so there is no Cato account to delete. If account features are added in the future, this policy will be updated and account deletion controls will be provided.

## Security

Cato uses app-private local storage for app data. Any diagnostic data sent to Sentry is transmitted using encrypted connections. You are responsible for the security of your device, Android backups, exported files, and any destination you choose when sharing exports.

## Children

Cato is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Cato's data practices change. The updated policy will be made available through the app and the Google Play listing.
