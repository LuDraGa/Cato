## Full Description

Cato is a calm, local-first daily self-tracking app for habits, mood, routines, and personal patterns.

Log quick daily entries, review your history in heatmaps, and use gentle local reminders to keep your routine visible without notification overload. Cato is designed for fast capture and quiet reflection: open the app, log what happened, and see your day update immediately.

What Cato includes:

- Daily habit and routine logging
- Mood, energy, count, score, duration, note, and media entry types
- Completion ring, daily score, and streak feedback
- Review heatmaps for seeing patterns over time
- Backfill and edit support for late entries
- Batch entry for day-end catchup
- Local reminders with a maximum of two notifications per day
- JSON export for your data
- Local-first storage with no account required

Cato stores your entries on your device for this release. It does not require login, cloud sync, subscriptions, or social sharing.

Cato is for personal self-tracking only. It is not a medical device, diagnosis tool, treatment service, or healthcare provider.
